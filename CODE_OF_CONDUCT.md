# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to maintaining a respectful and constructive environment for everyone contributing to this repository.

Expected behavior includes:

- Being respectful and professional.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Accepting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Focusing discussions on the project and its technical goals.
- Respecting other contributors' time, work, and boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Spam or unrelated promotional activity.
- Any conduct that would make the project unsafe or hostile for other contributors.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, commits, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project's community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related interactions.

By participating in the project, you agree to follow these standards.
